Access, income, and skills remain out of reach for too many

Africa's digital economy is growing fast — but the path into it is still narrow, uneven, and hard to navigate for most people.

Access

Millions remain disconnected from reliable digital tools, devices, and connectivity — the basic entry point to the digital economy.

Income

Digital work and earning opportunities exist, but they're scattered, hard to find, and often closed off to first-time entrants.

Skills

Training rarely connects directly to real income — leaving people credentialed but still without a clear path forward.
